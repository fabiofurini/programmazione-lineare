## Problema LP $P61$

!!! chiave ""

    $$
    \begin{array}{rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr}
    {\max} &  & {3\,x_1} & {+} & {\,x_2} & {+} & {2\,x_3} &  &  \\[2ex]
    \text{soggetto a} &  & {\,x_1} &  &  & {+} & {\,x_3} & {\le} & {5} \\[2ex]
    & {-} & {\,x_1} &  &  & {-} & {\,x_3} & {\le} & {-4} \\[2ex]
    & {-} & {\,x_1} & {+} & {2\,x_2} & {+} & {\,x_3} & {\le} & {5} \\[2ex]
    &  & {x_1,} &  & {x_2,} &  & {x_3} & { \ge} & { 0 }
